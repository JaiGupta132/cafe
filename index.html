Sullivan Coffee House — Better Coffee, Brighter Days

Sullivan Coffee House is a small café on Sullivan Street in Greenwich Village, pulling espresso in small batches and bringing in pastries fresh each morning. Come for the coffee, stay for the quiet.

On a quiet stretch of
Sullivan Street

Between the stoops and tree cover of Greenwich Village, Sullivan Coffee House keeps a short counter and an easy pace. We steep tea to order, pull espresso a cup at a time, and bring in croissants and muffins each morning for the walk to work or a long read by the window.

Our Story

Born in the
Hills, Loved by
Dehradun

AMA Cafe began as a beloved collaboration with the iconic Kalsang Restaurant, bringing together the warmth of trans-Himalayan culture and world-class café cuisine. Today, with two locations — our flagship on Rajpur Road and the Mall of Dehradun — we serve as Dehradun's favourite creative retreat.

Our name, our philosophy, our food — all rooted in the idea of "home." From matcha-infused signature pancakes to bold Caribbean spiced chicken, every dish is crafted with intention and love.
